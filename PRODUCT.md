# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Knowledge workers (developers, AI-agent power users) at a desk for long stretches. They run PawPal all day as an ambient companion: a transparent, always-on-top desktop pet that nudges them to take breaks, drink water, and stay focused, and that surfaces coding-agent activity (Codex, Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor) in thought bubbles. Context: the pet must never disrupt flow — nudges are gentle, glanceable, dismissable. The Settings dashboard is visited occasionally to tune reminders, review daily/monthly habit stats, and configure pets and agents.

## Product Purpose

A tiny desktop dog that helps you pause before you burn out. PawPal exists to make wellness nudges feel like a companion checking in, not a productivity tool issuing alerts. Success: users keep it running for weeks, actually take the breaks, and feel affection for the pet rather than annoyance at the reminders.

## Brand Personality

Warm, gentle, playful. Line Dog-inspired: soft, hand-drawn cuteness with calm delivery. The pet is the personality; the surrounding UI carries that warmth quietly through rounded shapes, cream surfaces, and friendly copy — never loud, never nagging.

## Design System Boundary

Each pet (Hachi, Xiao Ji Mao, White Line Dog, custom GIFs) is its own custom artwork and may differ freely. Everything else — Settings dashboard, thought/chat bubbles, context menus, popovers — follows one consistent shared design system. Bubble and settings styling must stay coherent regardless of which pet is active.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate SaaS dashboard**: no Linear/Stripe-style gray admin chrome, no sterile productivity-tool density or cold neutrals.
- **Generic Electron app**: no default-looking widgets, native-ish gray panels, or mismatched form controls that betray the framework.

## Design Principles

1. **Companion, not tool** — every surface should feel like the pet's world extending into the UI: warm surfaces, soft corners, friendly microcopy.
2. **Gentle by default** — nudges are glanceable and easily dismissed; no alarm colors, no guilt mechanics, no urgency theater.
3. **One bubble language** — thought bubbles, task bubbles, and popovers share a single visual vocabulary across all pets and agents.
4. **Never disrupt flow** — the pet overlay stays unobtrusive; motion is small and purposeful; nothing steals focus from the user's work.
5. **Charm with restraint** — cuteness lives in the pet art and small touches, not in decorated chrome; the settings UI is warm but functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Minimal formal requirements (personal app). Keep pragmatic basics: readable text contrast on cream surfaces, settings operable by keyboard, bilingual UI (English / Simplified Chinese) treated as first-class in all layouts.
